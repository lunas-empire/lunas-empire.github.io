## Editorial decisions

- Claims that vary between servers or releases tell members to check the live game UI.
- Store prices are intentionally omitted because price and stock can vary.
- Blueprint totals are intentionally omitted because requirements vary by star and release.
- Promotion scoring is conditional on the live event rules and Drill Ground capacity.
- Common event timing is described as usual practice, never as a fixed recurring schedule.
- Minister and Engineer skill percentages are not added together; active titles and timers must be verified before starting an action.

## RZSN General Trial policy

RZSN recommends selecting the highest unlocked difficulty. If an enemy exceeds a member's solo power, ask for rally help in alliance chat; stronger members can lead or join the rally to defeat it together. Share difficulty and enemy level so helpers find the target. This alliance-specific approach overrides the older generic advice to select an easier difficulty. Included in all 15 languages and regression-tested.
